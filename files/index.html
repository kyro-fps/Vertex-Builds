Our story

Five people.
One team.

We started Vertex because we kept seeing people pay too much for basic digital work — or give up because they couldn't afford an agency.

Gaming, studying, building websites, editing clips, making thumbnails, crunching data — between us, we cover the whole stack. And we keep prices honest.
